Who We Are

A COMMUNITY
OF INNOVATORS.

IEEE — the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers — is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

The IEEE Student Branch at RajaRajeswari College of Engineering (RRCE), Bengaluru, brings this global mission to campus — offering students a platform to learn, collaborate, compete and grow as the next generation of engineers and technologists.

Mission

To foster technical excellence, collaborative learning and professional growth among engineering students through IEEE's global network.

Vision

To cultivate a vibrant, innovation-driven student community at RRCE that contributes meaningfully to technology and society.

Community

A student-led space where engineers build skills through workshops, competitions, research, mentorship and inter-chapter collaboration.
